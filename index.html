A demo of redmine_tiptap, a WYSIWYG editor for Redmine: the editor of the plugin works right here, in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, and your text stays in this browser. There is no Redmine behind this page, so pictures you paste, drop or choose are kept only until the page is reloaded, and Redmine links and macros stay plain text in the preview.
